Applied principles: resolve follow-ups using dialogue context; distinguish social, research and analysis intentions; retrieve external knowledge; clarify consequential ambiguity; ground answers; evaluate multi-turn relevance and fluency. These are design principles, not proof of universal comprehension.

The Canadian official website remains the foundation. Parent-company filings and official government context may supplement it. Hypothetical outcomes are explicitly separated from facts. Current information depends on successful live research and must not be simulated when unavailable. Rankings require a metric and evidence. Search excerpts are partial evidence, not full document ingestion.
